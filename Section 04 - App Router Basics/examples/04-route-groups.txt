Route groups — parentheses folders are stripped from the URL

app/
├── (marketing)/
│   ├── layout.tsx        ← layout only for marketing pages
│   ├── page.tsx           →  /
│   └── about/
│       └── page.tsx        →  /about
└── (app)/
    ├── layout.tsx        ← different layout for app pages
    └── dashboard/
        └── page.tsx        →  /dashboard

Note: "(marketing)" and "(app)" do NOT appear in the URL.
Use route groups to give different sections of your site distinct
layouts/chrome without affecting the path.
