Catch-all vs. optional catch-all routes

app/docs/[...slug]/page.tsx
  matches:   /docs/a
             /docs/a/b
             /docs/a/b/c
  does NOT match: /docs   (no segment at all)
  params.slug is always an array: ["a", "b", "c"]

app/docs/[[...slug]]/page.tsx   (double brackets = optional)
  matches:   /docs
             /docs/a
             /docs/a/b
  params.slug is undefined for /docs, or an array otherwise
